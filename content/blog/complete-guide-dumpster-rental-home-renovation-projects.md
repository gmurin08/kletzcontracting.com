Capacity: Approximately 4 pickup truck loads

Ideal for:
- Kitchen renovations

- Basement cleanouts
- Single-room renovations
- Deck removal (up to 300 sq ft)

Our 12-yard dumpsters fit comfortably in most driveways and handle 80% of residential renovation projects perfectly.

**15-Yard Dumpsters: When You Need Extra Capacity**

Dimensions: 15' long x 8' wide x 4.5' high

Best suited for:
- Multi-room renovations
- Complete kitchen gut renovations
- Master suite remodels
- Garage cleanouts plus renovation
- Larger deck removals
- Partial home cleanouts
- Multiple flooring projects

The extra space provides breathing room for bigger projects without overwhelming residential properties.

✗ Tires
✗ Medical waste
✗ Propane tanks
✗ Oil and automotive fluids
✗ Refrigerators with freon

When in doubt, ask! We'll help identify proper disposal methods for restricted items.

## Cost-Saving Strategies from the Pros

**1. Right-Size Your Rental**
Choosing the correct size saves money:
- Too small = overflow fees or second rental
- Too large = wasted rental cost
- Just right = single flat fee, no surprises

**2. Plan Your Timeline**
Our 7-day rental period works for most projects. Strategic planning maximizes value:
- Schedule delivery for demolition start
- Coordinate trades to maintain workflow
- Complete disposal before final inspection
- Avoid weekend delivery fees when possible

**3. Load Efficiently**
Proper loading extends capacity:
- Break down large items
- Fill hollow items (bathtubs, sinks)
- Layer flat materials (drywall, plywood)
- Distribute weight evenly

**4. Separate Valuable Materials**
Remove before disposal:
- Scrap metal (sell to recyclers)
- Reusable fixtures (donate)
- Quality lumber (repurpose)
- Appliances in working condition

**5. Avoid Prohibited Items**
Contamination fees are expensive. Set aside:
- Paint cans for household hazardous waste days
- Electronics for e-waste recycling
- Chemicals for proper disposal

## Preparing for Dumpster Delivery

**Placement Considerations**
- Driveway placement: Most common, protects lawn
- Street placement: May require permit
- Clear access path: 10' width, 15' height clearance
- Level ground: Prevents rolling and damage
- Protection: Use plywood under bin to protect surface

**Day of Delivery Checklist**
□ Clear chosen area of vehicles and obstacles
□ Mark sprinkler heads and utilities
□ Remove low-hanging branches
□ Have plywood ready if protecting surface
□ Designate someone to meet driver
□ Confirm placement before driver leaves

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**1. Waiting Too Long to Reserve**
Peak season (May-September) books quickly. Reserve 1-2 weeks ahead.

**2. Underestimating Debris**
When in doubt, size up. The cost difference is minimal compared to overflow hassles.

**3. Ignoring Weight Limits**
Concrete, dirt, and roofing materials add up fast. Discuss heavy materials upfront.

**4. Poor Planning Access**
Measure twice! Ensure delivery trucks can access and maneuver safely.

**5. DIY Loading Hazards**
Improper loading causes injuries. Use ramps for heavy items, never throw debris from heights.
