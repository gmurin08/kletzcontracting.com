## What Makes James Hardie Different?

James Hardie isn't just another siding option—it's engineered specifically for long-term performance. The proprietary fiber cement composition combines:

- **Portland cement**: Provides strength and durability

This unique formula, developed over 30 years of research, creates siding that performs like no other material available.

## Comparing James Hardie to Other Siding Options

**James Hardie vs. Vinyl Siding**

*Durability*
- James Hardie: 30-50 year lifespan
- Vinyl: 10-20 year lifespan

*Weather Resistance*
- James Hardie: Withstands hail, won't melt
- Vinyl: Can crack in cold, melt from reflected heat

*Aesthetics*
- James Hardie: Authentic wood grain, holds paint
- Vinyl: Limited textures, color throughout

*Maintenance*
- James Hardie: Minimal, repainting possible
- Vinyl: Low maintenance but can't be painted

**James Hardie vs. Wood Siding**

*Durability*
- James Hardie: Rot-proof, insect-resistant
- Wood: Susceptible to rot, insects, weathering

*Maintenance*
- James Hardie: Paint lasts 15+ years
- Wood: Requires painting every 3-5 years

*Fire Resistance*
- James Hardie: Non-combustible
- Wood: Combustible, higher insurance rates

*Cost Over Time*
- James Hardie: Higher initial, lower lifetime cost
- Wood: Lower initial, high maintenance costs
